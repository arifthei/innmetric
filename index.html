The object

Observed. Approved. Changed. Verified. Rechecked.

InnMetric finds the defect, gets named approval, changes the agreed system and retests where the guest buys. That file is how a defect closes.

  1. Observed under recorded dates, occupancy, currency and channel conditions.
  2. Approved by a named hotel owner before any live change.
  3. Changed in the agreed PMS, channel manager, booking engine or OTA.
  4. Verified on the live path where the guest buys.
  5. Rechecked on the agreed schedule so the issue cannot quietly return.

Fit

For hotels that already have systems and still need an owner for the live result.

Strong fit

  • Independent hotels and resorts
  • Aparthotels with hotel-style distribution
  • Small local hotel groups
  • A direct booking engine plus two or more OTAs
  • An owner, GM or commercial lead who can approve access

Usually not a fit

  • Large international chains with centralized distribution teams
  • Individually listed homes that are not hotel-operated
  • Properties seeking only social media, advertising or review responses
  • Teams that cannot authorize access or name an approver
