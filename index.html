The gap

Platforms move the configuration they are given. They do not own the commercial result.

A hotel can show as connected while rooms are invisible on sellable dates, rate plans are mapped incorrectly, restrictions block demand, or a correction is later reversed. No single vendor is fully accountable for checking the guest-facing outcome across the stack.

Sellability

Inventory and price exist, but a stop-sell, stay restriction, or incomplete mapping keeps the room off a channel.

Rate structure

Derived rates drop, promotions stack, occupancy pricing drifts, or refundable and non-refundable plans fall out of relationship.

Control

A fix is undocumented, reversed, or recreated. The same problem returns and nobody can show what changed.

The control loop

Detect, evidence, approve, repair, verify, monitor.

We do not promise that every correction increases revenue. We promise a disciplined review, documented and approved changes, live verification, and a record of what was found, changed, and confirmed.

Baseline

Map the stack and test the live guest journey under recorded dates, occupancy, currency, and locale.

Evidence

Show what appeared on which surface, with conditions attached. Root cause stays unknown until we have authorized access.

Approved repair

Only the hotel approves live changes. High-risk work is logged with a rollback path.

Live check

We retest the guest-facing result and can keep watching for recurrence if the hotel wants ongoing control.

Fit

Built for independent hotels that cannot staff a full distribution team.

This is for

Independent urban hotels, serviced apartments, aparthotels, and small local groups. Typically 20–150 rooms. Direct sales plus at least two OTAs. A GM, owner, or revenue lead who can approve access.

This is not

  • A replacement for HotelRunner, SiteMinder, or your booking engine
  • A revenue-management robot or “AI auditor”
  • A marketing, SEO, or review-management agency
  • A fit for large international chains in this first cohort
