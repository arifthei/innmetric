The ownership gap

Several systems can work as designed while the hotel still sells the wrong offer.

Distribution defects rarely sit inside one screen. They emerge from the combined behavior of room types, rate plans, occupancy rules, restrictions, inventory, promotions, taxes and channel mappings. Each vendor sees part of the chain. The hotel is left to prove what happened then coordinate the correction.

A room exists but cannot be bought

Inventory appears open in one system while a stop sell, length-of-stay rule or incomplete mapping removes it from a channel.

The public offer drifts

Refundable and non-refundable rates lose their intended relationship. Occupancy pricing breaks. Promotions stack in a way nobody intended.

A fix does not stay fixed

A change is applied without a complete record. Another update reverses it. The original problem returns without a clear owner.

What InnMetric controls

The method

Detect. Evidence. Approve. Repair. Verify. Recheck.

Establish the baseline

We map the stack and test the live guest journey under recorded conditions.

Separate facts from hypotheses

We document what appeared publicly. Internal cause remains unknown until access confirms it.

Obtain named approval

The hotel decides which changes may be made. High-risk actions require an explicit rollback path.

Apply the correction

We update the agreed PMS, channel-manager, booking-engine or OTA configuration.

Verify the live result

We retest the guest-facing outcome. A back-office change is not treated as complete until the result is visible where the guest buys.

Recheck what matters

We revisit agreed outcomes on a defined cadence. Recurring work begins only after the baseline is understood.

Best fit

Built for hotels that need senior distribution control without another full-time department.

Strong fit

  • Independent hotels and resorts
  • Aparthotels with hotel-style distribution
  • Small local hotel groups
  • Properties selling through a direct booking engine plus two or more OTAs
  • Teams where the owner, GM or commercial lead can approve access and changes

Usually not a fit

  • Large international chains with centralized distribution teams
  • Individually listed homes that are not hotel-operated
  • Properties seeking only social media, advertising or review responses
  • Teams that cannot authorize access or name an approver
