# Website copy alternative

Prepared by Codex on 2026-09-07 after Mert requested a fresh review. This is the reasoning for a copy candidate, not a verdict from the branding agent or a new set of locked strings.

**Git baseline:** [`3e79c47df763a38053efe2b8a3db09454b9771fb`](https://github.com/arifthei/innmetric/commit/3e79c47df763a38053efe2b8a3db09454b9771fb), the latest `preview` commit when this review began. It supersedes the earlier comparison started against `61b4e34`. No alternative was committed during that interrupted attempt.

## The recommendation

Keep the shorter page. Give the reader enough information to choose the next step. The latest version correctly moves detailed diagnosis off the homepage, but its supporting copy removes the guide from the story. It asks the hotel to supply a stay before explaining what InnMetric will do with it.

The proposed paragraph restores that explanation in two sentences:

> InnMetric traces the problem through the systems you already use. We make the changes your hotel approves, check the booking again and leave a record your team can follow.

The useful end state is a checked booking and a record the next person can use. That gives the hotel a reason to start. We do not need to invent a night-shift anecdote, blame a vendor or promise recovered revenue to make the situation recognisable.

The implementation-suggestions document is more useful than mechanically applying the raw writing notes. It already rejects presumptive sales language and keeps the reader's competence intact. Its handover recommendation is the strongest part. Its repeated process paragraphs would make the current compact homepage longer, so the detail belongs in the existing Services and How it works sections.

The psychology claim needs a narrower reading. The original IKEA-effect working paper studies valuation of self-assembled boxes, origami and Lego models, with successful completion as a boundary condition. It does not test hotel enquiry forms or establish that friction improves conversion. My application is an editorial choice: ask for details needed to examine the booking, then make the request understandable. [Norton, Mochon and Ariely, The IKEA Effect, working paper](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/11-091.pdf).

## Head-to-head comparison

The current homepage H1, lede and buttons stay exactly as specified in Git: `The desk can quote the room. The guest still cannot buy it.`, `A successful push is not a sellable room.`, `Let's Solve It!` and form submit `Send the stay`. The older Drive locks are not restored.

| Area | Preview at 3e79c47 | Drive direction | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero support | Tells the reader the page will not diagnose the stay | Explain investigation through existing systems and approved changes | The two-sentence paragraph above names the service and the handover without diagnosing the visitor's hotel |
| First contribution | Fragmented request for the hotel and expected stay | Ask for the hotel, observation and expected result | `Start with the hotel, room and dates. Tell us what you expected to see.` |
| Closing invitation | `That is the first piece of work. It has to come from you.` | A specific invitation followed by fit and next step | `Tell us what you expected to see and what appeared instead. We'll explain whether it fits our work and what the next step would be.` |
| Repair offer | `Approved stays become bookable again.` | Approved changes followed by a check | `Agreed corrections, checked against the same booking.` The Services paragraph records work still needing attention instead of promising success |
| Rate example | Compares the OTA price with the desk's quote tonight | Illustrative symptoms without a diagnosis | `A guest finds a different rate online. Your team needs to know whether the offers match.` Different conditions may explain the price |
| Recurring issue | Attributes recurrence to a later update | Keep situations illustrative and avoid unsupported causes | The booking works after a fix and fails on the next check. The copy does not claim to know why |
| Process | Three phases, beginning with work that must come from the hotel | Clarify scope, trace the issue, check and hand over | Retain three phases and give each a practical result. Explicitly retain unresolved items and agreed rechecks |
| Contact | Says InnMetric will reply while noting that the form does not send | A short form and truthful acceptance/failure states | Retain the four enquiry fields, optional role and consent. Define the booking example in the placeholder and direct current enquiries to the existing email address. No form wiring or false receipt |
| Search descriptions | A short hook or repeated exclusions | Explain the service before asking the reader to interpret terminology | Concise descriptions of investigation, approved correction and the record. The exclusions stay in the FAQ and service limits |

No ticket row, five-step homepage process or extra FAQ is added. The four-card wheel, offer names, wrong-fit column, three process phases and existing form structure remain. About keeps the existing founder biographies. The copy distinguishes completing a booking from completing a hotel stay.

## Product-limit review requested by Cursor

The scope decisions in the six search answers are consistent with Intent. Two answers needed more careful wording. An unavailable booking result does not establish its cause, and an investigation does not guarantee that one setting will resolve it.

| Search question | Review and resulting boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Empty booking-engine availability | Keep the distinction between an unavailable engine and a room/date mismatch. Replace the requirement to know whether every hotel on the engine is affected. Review relevant settings without declaring the cause from a public result. |
| Different Booking.com rates | Compare matching room, dates, occupancy, terms, currency and promotions. No vendor accusation or automatic parity fault. |
| Channel-manager setup | Review an existing live system and propose corrections for approval. No first-time installation or guarantee that we already know the setting. |
| Rate-plan simplification | May be a finding within the review and needs hotel approval. No standalone redesign offer. |
| Outsourced revenue management | Decline the service. The hotel retains pricing, yield and rate decisions. |
| OTA listing management | Decline content, photos and ranking work. Scheduled checks after a baseline cover agreed rates, availability and mappings. |

The prior review threads can close with these findings. Cursor's recorded browser checks belong to the earlier commit; they do not establish that the new wording fits every viewport. The new review request is limited to the revised copy and its wrapping.
